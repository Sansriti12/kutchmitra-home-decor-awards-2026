# Design Direction

Premium, editorial, architectural and aspirational.

Reference inspiration may be used for information hierarchy and user journey only.
Do not copy another site's branding, text or proprietary content.

Preferred:
- deep navy/deep blue
- gold accents
- white/warm neutral backgrounds
- large architectural/interior photography
- strong Nominate Now CTA
- clean multi-step wizard
- mobile-first layouts
- accessible typography
- subtle animation
